## Setup

1. Shuffle all cards to form a facedown draw deck.
2. Turn over the top two cards to start **two discard piles**.
3. Pick a random first player.

---

## Playing a Turn

Each turn has three steps.

### 1. Take a card (mandatory)

Choose **one**:

- **Draw from the deck:** take the top 2 cards and keep 1. Put the other faceup on either discard pile.
- **Take from a discard pile:** take the top card of either pile.

You may **not** look through the discard piles.

### 2. Play duos (optional)

Place pairs of duo cards faceup in front of you to use their effects. You can play several pairs in one turn.

| Pair | Effect |
|---|---|
| 2 × Crab | Look through one discard pile and take any card from it. |
| 2 × Boat | Take another turn right away. |
| 2 × Fish | Add the top card of the deck to your hand. |
| Swimmer + Shark | Steal a random card from another player's hand. |

### 3. End the round (optional)

If your cards (in hand **and** in front of you) are worth **7 points or more**, you may end the round. If you don't, play passes to the left.

---

## Ending a Round

The player who ends the round announces one of two calls.

### STOP

All players reveal their hands, and everyone scores their card points.

### LAST CHANCE

Every other player gets **one last turn**. Then all hands are revealed.

- **Caller has the highest total (or is tied):** the caller scores card points **plus** the color bonus. Opponents score **only** their color bonus.
- **Someone beats the caller:** the caller scores **only** the color bonus. Opponents score their card points.

**Color bonus:** points equal to the number of cards you have in your most common color.

### Empty deck

If the deck runs out at the end of a turn, the round ends right away and **nobody scores**.

### Next round

The player to the left of whoever ended the last round starts the next one.

---

## Scoring

### Duo cards

- **1 point per pair**, whether the pair is in front of you or still in your hand.

### Collector cards

Points depend on how many of the same type you have:

| Card | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shell | 0 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Octopus | 0 | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | — |
| Penguin | 1 | 3 | 5 | — | — | — |
| Sailor | 0 | 5 | — | — | — | — |

### Multiplier cards

| Card | Points |
|---|---|
| Lighthouse | 1 per Boat |
| Shoal of Fish | 1 per Fish |
| Penguin Colony | 2 per Penguin |
| Captain | 3 per Sailor |

### Mermaids

- Your first mermaid scores 1 point for each card of your **most common color**. Your second mermaid scores your next most common color, and so on.
- Mermaids count as **white**.
- **Having all 4 mermaids wins the game immediately.**

---

## End of the Game

- **Instant win:** a player places all 4 mermaids.
- Otherwise, the game ends when someone reaches the target score:

| Players | Target score |
|---|---|
| 2 | 40 |
| 3 | 35 |
| 4 | 30 |

The **highest score** wins. If there's a tie, the tied player who scored the most points in the last round wins.
