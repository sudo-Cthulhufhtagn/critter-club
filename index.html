How to play

Goal: first to the target score wins (2 players: 40, 3: 35, 4: 30).

Your turn

  1. Get a card: draw 2 from the deck, keep 1 and put the other face up on a discard pile (an empty pile must be used) — or take the top card of a discard pile.
  2. Play pairs (optional, any number): two matching Foxes, Rabbits or Capybaras — or a Raccoon + Badger — go face up in front of you and do their trick. Fox: take any card from one pile. Rabbit: extra turn. Capybara: draw the top of the deck. Raccoon + Badger: steal a random card. Each pair is worth 1 point whether played or held.
  3. End: if your cards are worth 7+ you may Stop or call Last Chance; otherwise end your turn.

Ending a round

Stop: the round ends now; everyone scores their cards.

Last Chance: everyone else gets one more turn. If your card score is then at least as high as everyone else's, you score cards + color bonus and they score only their color bonus. If not, you score only your color bonus and they score their cards.

Color bonus = how many of your cards (hand + played) share your most common color.

If the deck is empty when you have to draw, the round ends and nobody scores.

Deer

Each Deer scores your next-largest colour group (Deer count as black & white). Collect all four Deer and you win the game instantly.

Colors

Every card has a color, shown by its swatch and a marker (shape or letters) so you never need to rely on hue. The color bonus is the number of your cards in your most common color. Each Deer (a mermaid, counted as Black & White) scores your next-largest color group. The four unique colors belong to one multiplier card each.
